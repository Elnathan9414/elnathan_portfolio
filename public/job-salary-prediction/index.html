Job Salary Prediction

Salary trend exploration based on professional role, experience, skills, and employment factors that influence compensation.

Objective

This report explores salary prediction by analyzing job-related variables such as professional role, experience level, skills, and employment characteristics.

Key Insights

The dashboard makes it easier to compare salary trends across job profiles and identify the factors that appear to have the strongest influence on compensation.

Business Value

This analysis can support career planning, recruitment strategy, compensation benchmarking, and workforce decision-making.
